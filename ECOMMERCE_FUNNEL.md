# E-commerce Funnel Benchmarks Guide

In e-commerce, these benchmarks help you measure how efficiently users move through your sales funnel—from browsing to buying.

---

## 1. Add-to-Cart Rate

**Definition:**  
Percentage of visitors who add at least one product to their cart.

**Formula:**
```
Add-to-Cart Rate = (Sessions with Add to Cart / Total Sessions) × 100
```

**What it measures:**  
How compelling your product pages and offers are.

**Typical Benchmarks (general ecommerce):**
- **Low:** < 5%
- **Average:** 5–10%
- **Strong:** 10–15%
- **Excellent:** 15%+

**If low, possible issues:**
- Weak product images/descriptions
- Poor pricing / trust signals
- Slow page speed
- Bad mobile UX
- Wrong traffic targeting

---

## 2. Cart Abandonment Rate

**Definition:**  
Percentage of users who add items to cart but leave without purchasing.

**Formula:**
```
Cart Abandonment Rate = ((Carts Created - Purchases) / Carts Created) × 100
```

**What it measures:**  
Checkout friction and purchase hesitation.

**Typical Benchmarks:**
- **Industry Average:** 60–80%
- **Good:** < 65%
- **Excellent:** < 50%

**Why users abandon carts:**
- Unexpected shipping costs
- Forced account creation
- Complicated checkout
- Payment issues
- "Just browsing" / price comparing

---

## 3. Overall Conversion Rate

**Definition:**  
Percentage of total visitors who complete a purchase.

**Formula:**
```
Conversion Rate = (Orders / Total Sessions) × 100
```

**What it measures:**  
Overall effectiveness of your entire funnel.

**Typical Benchmarks:**
- **Low:** < 1%
- **Average:** 1–3%
- **Strong:** 3–5%
- **Excellent:** 5%+

---

## How They Connect in the Funnel

Think of the funnel like this:

```
1000 Visitors
   ↓
100 Add to Cart (10% ATC)
   ↓
30 Purchase (70% Cart Abandonment)
   ↓
3% Overall Conversion
```

---

## How to Interpret Them Together

### High Add-to-Cart + High Abandonment
Usually means:
- Product interest is strong
- Checkout process is the problem

### Low Add-to-Cart + Low Conversion
Usually means:
- Product/traffic mismatch
- Weak merchandising / product pages

### Good Add-to-Cart + Good Abandonment + Low Conversion
Could mean:
- Not enough traffic
- Tracking issues
- Funnel leakage elsewhere

---

## Funnel Benchmark by Store Maturity (Rough Guide)

| Metric | Early Store | Healthy Store | Elite Store |
|--------|------------|---------------|-------------|
| Add-to-Cart | 5–8% | 8–12% | 12–20%+ |
| Cart Abandonment | 70–80% | 60–70% | <50–60% |
| Conversion Rate | 1–2% | 2–4% | 5%+ |

---

## Important Caveat

Benchmarks vary heavily by:
- Industry / niche
- Price point
- Traffic source (organic vs ads)
- Device type (mobile lower than desktop)
- Geography
- New vs returning customers

> A luxury furniture store and a low-ticket cosmetics store will have very different benchmarks.

---

## Pro Tip for Product/Analytics Teams

Don't optimize only overall conversion.  
Break funnel into stages:

1. **Product Page View Rate**
2. **Add-to-Cart Rate**
3. **Checkout Start Rate**
4. **Payment Success Rate**
5. **Purchase Rate**

This helps isolate bottlenecks.
